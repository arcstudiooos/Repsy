# Kinetic Sprint 5 Implementation Plan — Pilot-ready R1

**Goal:** The coach sees honest counts (adherence with numerator, denominator and period), coaches and clients export their data, deletion requests run through a verified, audited job from a platform-admin console with explicit support access, product events reach analytics without personal data, and the app is hardened for the pilot (accessibility, performance, security, docs).

**Spec:** `docs/implementation-plan.md` Sprint 5 tickets S5-1…S5-5 (Target data model rows `ExportJob`, `DeletionRequest`, `SupportAccessGrant`), as amended by `docs/decisions.md` (D-006 roles, D-007, D-008 #1/#16/#17, clar. 1/4, D-010 #4), plus the Sprint 5 section of `docs/sprint-4-followups.md`. Where they conflict, decisions.md wins.

**Definition of done (sprint):** pilot launch checklist (§17) items that code can satisfy; every journey E2E (A–E) green; per task `pnpm lint && pnpm typecheck && pnpm format:check && pnpm test`, plus `pnpm test:e2e` for UI tasks.

## Rulings made while planning

1. **Adherence** (D-008 clar. 1) = completed ÷ eligible, where eligible = sessions whose due window (`dueAt + graceHours`) ended inside the period, excluding `cancelled` and sessions of a paused period (shifted sessions are simply not due then; sessions of a client paused for the whole period don't exist in it). Skipped and incomplete stay in the denominator and are reported separately. Period = local dates `from`–`to` inclusive, by the session's `scheduledLocalDate`. A zero denominator shows "No sessions due yet".
2. **Response rate** = check-ins sent ÷ check-ins due (due window ended) in the period. **Time to follow-up** = median hours from a flag opening to a coach resolving or dismissing it (automatic resolutions excluded), for flags closed in the period.
3. **Exports.** `POST /api/v1/exports` with `{ scope: 'org' | 'client_self', idempotencyKey }` creates one `ExportJob` per key. The worker writes one CSV object per resource under `exports/{orgId}/{jobId}/` and records the file list on the job last. The download endpoint re-checks the requester on every call and hands out a link valid 15 minutes (S3: presigned GET; local: a signed app URL). Failed jobs keep no partial objects visible (objects are only listed in the manifest written last).
4. **Storage** goes through `StoragePort` (`put`, `signedGetUrl`, `delete`). Locally and in tests a filesystem adapter (like the email dev sink); in production S3-compatible (`S3_*` env).
5. **Deletion.** A client deletes their own data in one organization; an owner may request deletion of a client of theirs. A platform admin verifies, then runs the job. The job removes the subject's rows **within that relationship** (sessions, set logs and revisions, check-ins, messages they sent and their thread, flags, notes about them, pauses, invites, their client copy programs) and anonymizes the client row; it never touches other memberships of the same user. `legalHold` blocks it. Audit is content-free. Rerunning a done job is a no-op.
6. **Support access.** A platform admin reads a client's data only with an unexpired `SupportAccessGrant` (reason required, 24h). Without one → 403. Every grant and read is audited.
7. **Analytics** go through `AnalyticsPort` (PostHog adapter, no-op without `POSTHOG_KEY`). Events and properties are an allowlist (§13); a property outside it throws in tests. `client_id_hash` = HMAC-SHA256(`ANALYTICS_HASH_SECRET`, clientId). Never names, free text, answers or bodies.
