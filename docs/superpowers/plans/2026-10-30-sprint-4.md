# Kinetic Sprint 4 Implementation Plan — Complete coaching workflow

**Goal:** Close the coaching loop: weekly check-ins issued and answered, workouts that weren't done become `missed` / `incomplete`, a rules engine raises "Needs review" flags into a review queue the coach can act on (resolve, dismiss, snooze, message, follow up), 1:1 messaging, and a client history timeline with private coach notes.

**Spec:** `docs/implementation-plan.md` Sprint 4 tickets S4-1…S4-5 (Target data model rows `CheckinTemplate`, `Checkin`, `ReviewFlag`, `ConversationThread`, `Message`, `InternalNote`), as amended by `docs/decisions.md` (D-003, D-005 review-queue timing, D-007, D-008 #1/#4/#5/#9/#10/#11, D-010 #3), plus the Sprint 4 sections of `docs/sprint-2-followups.md` and `docs/sprint-3-followups.md`. Where they conflict, decisions.md wins.

**Definition of done (sprint):** full closed-loop E2E (`closed-loop.spec.ts`, `journey-d.spec.ts`) plus every earlier spec; per task `pnpm lint && pnpm typecheck && pnpm format:check && pnpm test`, and `pnpm test:e2e` for UI tasks. Global constraints, verbatim copy rules and environment rules of the Sprint 3 plan still apply.

## Rulings made while planning

1. **One check-in template per organization** (R1 organizations have one coach, D-006), created from the seeded questions the first time it is read. Editing it never changes an issued check-in: each `Checkin` keeps a `questionsJson` snapshot (an addition to the target model, so answers always match the questions asked).
2. **Issuing.** `checkins.issue` (every 10 min) issues a client's check-in when the client is `active` (never `paused`, `invited` or `archived`), has a check-in weekday, today in the client's zone is that weekday, the local time is 06:00 or later, and the client has no check-in in the 6 days before today. The last condition makes a weekday change apply from the next period. `issuedAt` = 06:00 local, `dueAt` = 23:59 local. Generation is idempotent on `(clientId, periodLocalDate)`. The "check-in ready" email goes through the outbox with idempotency key `checkin-ready:{checkinId}`.
3. **Check-in submit** is idempotent on `idempotencyKey`: the same key returns the stored submission; a different key after submission → 409 `CONFLICT`. Every question is optional; answers are validated by type (`scale_1_5` integer 1–5, `short_text` ≤ 2,000 chars, `yes_no` boolean). A check-in can still be submitted after its due time.
4. **Derived session states** (`review.sweep`, every 10 min): `scheduled` past `dueAt + graceHours` → `missed` and locked to its slot's version at that moment (Sprint 2 follow-up); `in_progress` past it → `incomplete`. A late sync may still log and complete an `incomplete` session, and a `missed` one may still be started; a late completion re-evaluates Rule 1.
5. **Flags.** `dedupeKey` = `missed_workouts:{clientId}:{firstSessionId}:{secondSessionId}`, `checkin_overdue:{checkinId}`, `checkin_new:{checkinId}`, `message_unread:{messageId}`, `coach_followup:{uuid}`. A flag whose condition no longer holds is auto-resolved (`resolvedBy` null, reason "Resolved automatically"). Snoozed flags reopen when `snoozedUntil` passes (sweep), keeping their source due date. Rule 4 counts only messages from the client, unread by the coach for more than 24h, and only when the coach opted in (`NotificationPreference.messageEmailOptIn` doubles as the opt-in, D-008 clar. 3).
6. **Messages.** One thread per client, created on first open. A message is read when the recipient lists the thread. Archived clients can't post (409), and their coach can read but not post. The opt-in email never contains the body.
7. **Internal notes** are coach/owner only; any portal (client) request is 403 and no portal payload ever includes them. Only the author may edit or delete a note.
