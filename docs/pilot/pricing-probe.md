# Pricing probe ($29–$49 per month)

Run outside the app (D-008 #12: no in-app checkout). Goal: learn what pilot coaches would pay, not to sell.

1. At the week-5 check-in call, after the time-on-task observation, ask: "If Kinetic cost $29 a month for up to 30 clients, would you keep using it after the pilot?" Then the same at $39 and $49 (ask in that order; stop at the first "no").
2. Ask what would have to be true to pay $49.
3. Record answers per coach (no client data). Do not negotiate or promise features.
