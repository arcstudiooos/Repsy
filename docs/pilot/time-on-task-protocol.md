# Time-on-task protocol: weekly review of 20 clients

**Question:** does Kinetic cut the time a coach spends on their weekly review of 20 clients by at least 30% against their current way of working?

## Baseline (before onboarding, week 0)

1. With the coach's consent, observe one normal weekly review in their current tools (spreadsheets, messaging apps, other software). Screen-share or in person.
2. Start the clock when they begin reviewing clients; stop when they say they're done with the review (not including programming new blocks).
3. Note per client: time, what they looked at, what action they took (message, program change, nothing).
4. Count clients reviewed. Normalize to 20 clients: `baseline_minutes × 20 / clients_reviewed`.

## Kinetic (weeks 3 and 5)

Repeat the observation in Kinetic, starting from `/app` (dashboard) and the review queue. Same start/stop rules. Note which screens they used and where they hesitated.

**Target met** when the week-5 time is ≤ 70% of baseline for the majority of pilot coaches. Report the median and every individual result; no averaging away outliers.

## Ethics and data

Observation notes stay outside the product and contain no client names (use client initials). Coaches can stop at any time.
