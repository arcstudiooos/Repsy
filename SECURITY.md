# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately to **security@repsy.fit** (or through GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" on this repository). Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact you expect. We will:

- acknowledge your report within 3 working days;
- keep you updated, and tell you when it is fixed;
- credit you if you would like.

## Safe harbour

We will not take legal action against research done in good faith that:

- uses only test accounts you create, and never accesses, changes or deletes other people's data (stop and tell us if you reach any);
- avoids degrading the service (no denial-of-service, spam or social engineering);
- gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before any disclosure.

## Scope

The Repsy web app and API, and this repository. Third-party services we use (hosting, email, storage) should be reported to those providers.

More about how we protect data: [docs/security](docs/security/README.md).
